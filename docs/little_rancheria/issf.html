Step selection function

Objective

Estimate and map connectivity between seasonal ranges for caribou using:

  • GPS telemetry,
  • integrated step-selection functions (iSSF),
  • landcover and burn-history covariates,
  • and resistance-based corridor modeling.

9. Fit integrated Step Selection Function (iSSF)

Example model:

movement kernel: - step length - turning angle

habitat selection: - forest cover proportion - distance to fooprint - distance to burned area

10. Interpret coefficients

Typical interpretation:

  • positive burn coefficient:
    • selection for burned areas
  • negative burn coefficient:
    • avoidance of burns
  • landcover coefficients:
    • relative selection among vegetation types
